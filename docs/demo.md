# Demo

Use a real account on the deployed app. Do not prepare the database first.

## Workflow

1. Create an account.
2. Start a real conversation.
3. Develop an idea across the chat.
4. Make a decision in your own words.
5. Reject a direction in your own words.
6. Open **Current thinking** from the conversation header. Wait for the "Current thinking updated" note under a reply, then check that the decision and the set-aside direction are there with your reasons.
7. Correct one section in your own words and save it.
8. Wrap up ("thanks, I have what I need"). Accept the draft Frimz offers, or name a format.
9. Leave and come back in a new conversation.
10. Ask Frimz to continue the idea.
11. Ask why a direction was rejected.
12. Ask how the idea arrived where it is. Open the idea page and compare the timeline and the versions of current thinking.
13. Open Memory, then edit or forget something you actually want changed.

## Acceptance

Memory is doing real work only if the later conversation is different because of stored context.

```text
WITHOUT the prior memory
  The reply has to ask which idea you mean.

WITH Frimz recall and current thinking
  The reply continues the actual idea from where the brief says it stands
  It can explain a stored rejection
  It can answer how the idea evolved from stored events
  It keeps the section you corrected in your words
  When a working-style preference exists, the reply follows it
```

The brief should also pass these checks:

- A suggestion Frimz made, and you never accepted, appears as a suggestion or an option. It never appears as your decision.
- Changing direction moves the earlier direction into "How it got here".
- The draft uses only what the conversation, memories, and brief contain. Anything uncertain is labelled as an assumption or an open question.

If the history does not contain a step, Frimz should say it does not have that step. Do not fill a missing step for the recording. Do not edit a brief to stage a recording; every brief on screen must come from the conversation being shown.

## Before recording

Confirm the memories on screen were extracted from the conversation you are about to show. Confirm the other person's permission if their words will be published.
